# 🌱 Community Code of Conduct

This repository is a learning environment for data science and machine learning. Contributors should be able to ask questions, discuss methods, challenge results, and improve notebooks without personal hostility.

## 🤝 Our commitment

We aim to foster respectful, constructive, and inclusive collaboration regardless of technical experience or background.

## ✅ Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique analysis and code, not people.
- Explain technical feedback with evidence.
- Welcome genuine beginner questions.
- Respect privacy and dataset restrictions.
- Credit sources and contributions accurately.
- Be honest about model results and limitations.
- Distinguish hypotheses from established findings.

## 🚫 Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against good-faith reporters.
- Deliberate disruption or spam.
- Sharing identifiable patient information.
- Misrepresenting an educational model as clinically validated.

## 💬 Scientific disagreement

Different preprocessing choices, models, and metrics are appropriate in different circumstances. Discuss assumptions, data, validation strategy, and evidence. A result should not be defended solely because it produced a higher score.

## 📣 Reporting concerns

Contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md instead. Avoid posting sensitive personal or health information in public discussions.

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern in good faith is prohibited.
